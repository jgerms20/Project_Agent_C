# 2026-02-26 - Deep Interview: Protagonist, GSP, W+K Layoff, MAIP

## Raw Input

### Protagonist Identity (90/10 Real to Invented)
- Initially said 70/30, then corrected to basically 90% real
- "Some of the scenarios are changed, but yeah, it's me"
- The character IS Joshua. Not a vessel, not a starting point — it's him with creative license on scenarios
- This means the show is essentially autobiography with fictionalized names/settings

### GSP - Never Promoted (The Goalpost Story)
- Had direct conversations about advancement, but they **constantly moved the goalposts**
- Classic placating: "You could do a little better" was the refrain
- Initially thought it was a personal thing, then realized it was **systemic** — especially for strategy roles
- Other people experienced the same treatment
- "Underestimating" — coded language about what they value and what that "looks like"
- "We know what we value and what that looks like" — i.e., a specific archetype that doesn't include him
- This is THE cognitive dissonance engine of Season 1
- **Show implication**: The audience thinks it's personal, then slowly realizes it's the system

### W+K Layoff - Relief Then Rage
- First feeling: RELIEF — "finally free"
- The rage came later — processing mythology vs. reality
- **He was already done before they fired him**
- Was literally journaling 1-2 days before about wanting something different
- Told his girlfriend days before that he didn't know his feelings about what was coming
- Then it happened — "a clarity situation"
- **The journal entry is a real artifact** — he may share it

### W+K Spell Break (Multiple Factors)
1. **Seeing the Kool-Aid drinking** — realizing how bought-in everyone else was
2. **"I don't base my whole personality off of a workplace"** — the outsider clarity
3. **The Portland of it all** — being Black in that space, the contradictions of progressive Portland
4. **The silos** — people only hung out with other W+K people, insular social world

### MAIP - Both Gift and Trap
- MAIP is **both the gift and the trap** — this tension is CENTRAL to the show
- "It opened the door but also made me the token success story they could point to"
- The protagonist owes his career to a diversity pipeline that the industry weaponizes as proof of progress
- He's simultaneously grateful and used
- This is the thematic engine of the entire series
