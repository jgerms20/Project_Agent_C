# Episode Outline

## When to Use
When the creator wants to develop an episode concept — from a rough idea to a structured outline.

## Instructions

1. **Clarify the episode seed** — what's the core idea, scene, or conflict?
2. **Identify placement** — which season, which agency era, rough position in the season
3. **Develop using the template below** — work iteratively with the creator
4. **Check thematic alignment** against `docs/series-bible/03-themes.md`
5. **Save the outline** to `scripts/episodes/SXX-EXX-title.md`
6. **Update season arcs** in `docs/series-bible/04-season-arcs.md`
7. **Update the session log**

## Episode Outline Template

```markdown
# S[X]E[XX] - "[Episode Title]"

## Metadata
- **Season**: X | **Episode**: XX
- **Agency Era**: GSP / W+K / TBWA
- **Tone**: [comedic / dramatic / surreal / mixed — describe]
- **Runtime Target**: ~30 min
- **Standalone vs. Arc**: [How this episode works alone AND feeds the season arc]

## Logline
[One sentence — what happens in this episode]

## Central Theme / Question
[What is this episode really about beneath the plot?]

## Cold Open
[1-2 sentences — how the episode starts, what hooks the viewer]

## Act One (Setup)
[The normal world of this episode. What's the situation? Who's involved?]

## Act Two (Confrontation)
[The complication. Things escalate. The protagonist (or POV character) faces the core conflict.]

## Act Three (Resolution / Shift)
[How it resolves — or doesn't. What has changed? What's the lingering feeling?]

## Key Scenes
- [Scene 1 description]
- [Scene 2 description]
- [Scene 3 description]

## Character Beats
- **Protagonist**: [What do they learn, experience, or lose in this episode?]
- **Supporting**: [Which characters feature and what do they do?]

## AI Easter Eggs / Thread
[Any AI-related plants or developments in this episode, even subtle ones]

## Music Cues
[Any specific songs or sonic mood for this episode]

## Notes
[Anything else — real memories it's based on, visual ideas, dialogue snippets]
```

## Model Usage
- Use **Sonnet** for drafting outlines
- Use **Opus** for evaluating how the episode fits the season arc and series themes
- Use **Haiku** for formatting and template filling
