# World Build

## When to Use
When the creator wants to develop the world of the show — agency culture, industry dynamics, city vibes, time period details, or the broader cultural landscape.

## Instructions

1. **Identify what aspect of the world** is being developed:
   - Agency-specific culture and dynamics
   - City/setting details (SF, Portland, LA)
   - Industry-wide context (trends, events, culture)
   - Cultural/societal context (BLM, Covid, AI, music, politics)
   - The advertising process itself (how work gets made)

2. **Research if needed** — use web search for real industry events, agency history, cultural timeline

3. **Develop the world detail** with creator input

4. **Update the appropriate docs**:
   - Agency specifics → `docs/research/agencies/[agency].md`
   - World overview → `docs/series-bible/01-world.md`
   - Cultural context → can go in world doc or themes doc depending on relevance

5. **Cross-reference with themes** — every world detail should connect to at least one core theme

6. **Update session log**

## World-Building Prompts (for interviewing the creator)

### Agency Culture
- What did a typical day look like?
- What was the office/workspace like? (or the remote equivalent)
- What were the unwritten rules everyone followed?
- What was the agency's self-mythology? (What did they tell themselves about who they were?)
- What was the gap between the self-mythology and reality?
- Who had the real power vs. the official power?

### City/Setting
- What did the city feel like during this era?
- What neighborhood were you in? What was around?
- What was the commute/daily routine?
- How did the city affect your work and mindset?
- What did the city represent to you at that time?

### Industry Context
- What was the big conversation in advertising during this period?
- What campaigns or moments defined the era?
- What was changing and who was resisting the change?
- What did clients want? What were agencies selling?

## Model Usage
- Use **Sonnet** for research and writing
- Use **Opus** for synthesizing world details into thematic meaning
- Use **Haiku** for fact-checking and quick lookups
