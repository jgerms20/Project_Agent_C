Agency Research

Background research on the three real agencies that inspire the show

Goodby Silverstein & Partners (GSP)

Season 1: The Awakening

Real Agency Facts

Founded

1983 by Jeff Goodby and Rich Silverstein

Location

San Francisco, CA

Size

Mid-size independent (vs. holding company agencies)

Reputation

One of the most respected independent agencies. Creative excellence. Smart, sophisticated work.

Notable Campaigns

  • Got Milk?
  • BMW Films
  • Doritos
  • Frito-Lay
  • Pepsi
  • HP
  • Adobe
  • NBA

Creator's Experience (2020–2023)

Entry Through MAIP internship program → brought on full-time
Role Junior Strategist (entire tenure — never promoted)
Work Mode Entirely remote. Never lived in SF.
Duration ~3 years (2020 to April/May 2023)

Agency Culture

  • Sophisticated, academic energy — "Very, very smart" intellectual culture
  • Peak performativeness during Covid + BLM — Peak statements of diversity and inclusion during societal reckoning
  • Contradictory messaging — Says diversity, contradicts in practice
  • Creator's perspective — Describes as "eyes wide open" awakening

Key Stories & Incidents

The Vaccine Incident

Creator was unvaccinated, denied office access. A story of exclusion disguised as policy—a microcosm of institutional gatekeeping.

BLM Response

Agency's public statements vs. internal reality during George Floyd protests. The gap between public allyship and private action.

Never Promoted

Junior strategist the entire tenure at a prestigious agency—what happened? A story of stagnation and institutional limits.

Remote Disconnect

Experiencing a "dream agency" entirely through screens. The distance between myth and reality.

Entry Story

Getting the MAIP notification, the feeling of making it. The promise at the beginning.

Show Themes Most Present

  • Cognitive Dissonance — Saying one thing, doing another
  • Performativeness — DEI theater and virtue signaling
  • Allyship vs. Alliance — The spectrum from fake to real
  • Cultural + Societal Shifts — Post-2020 reckoning

Research Needs

  • GSP's actual public statements during BLM (2020)
  • GSP's diversity/inclusion initiatives and outcomes
  • Agency leadership during this period
  • Notable campaigns/work during 2020–2023
  • Any public layoffs or restructuring
  • Jeff Goodby's public persona and role during this era

  • Fictionalized agency name TBD
  • How closely to mirror the real agency
  • Which details are changed, which are kept

Wieden+Kennedy (W+K)

Season 2: The Cult

Real Agency Facts

Founded

1982 by Dan Wieden and David Kennedy

Location

Portland, OR (HQ), with offices worldwide

Size

The largest independently owned agency globally

Reputation

Arguably the most famous advertising agency in the world. Creative legend. Independent.

Culture

Famously intense. Creative-first. Strong internal mythology.

Notable Campaigns

  • Nike "Just Do It"
  • Old Spice
  • Coca-Cola
  • Ford
  • McDonald's

Creator's Experience (2023–2024)

Entry Hired as Senior / Comms & Media Supervisor
Location Portland — first time being in-person at an agency
Duration ~1 year (2023–2024)
Exit Laid off — major story beat

Agency Culture

  • Height of cult-like vibes — "Buy in or opt out" mentality is explicit
  • Corporate culture masquerading as creative revolution — The agency's mythology is powerful and deliberate
  • Nike as crown jewel — The Nike relationship defines the agency's identity and power
  • "Keep Portland Weird" extends inward — The culture is specific, local, and insular

Key Stories & Incidents

First Time In-Person

After years of remote work at GSP, finally being physically in an agency. The sensory reality of it—the people, the energy, the mythology made flesh.

The Cult Buy-In

How W+K's mythology works on you. The history, the Nike of it all, the sense of being inside the most famous agency in the world.

Portland Culture Shock

A Black Southerner navigating "Keep Portland Weird"—the contradictions, the progressive aesthetics, the actual dynamics.

The Layoff

How it happened, when, who else, the feeling, the aftermath. A rupture in the mythology.

The Work

What was created, what mattered, what got killed by clients and politics. The gap between creative ambition and corporate reality.

Belonging and Alienation

The high of being inside the most famous agency, the simultaneous outsider feeling. Ascension and exile.

Show Themes Most Present

  • Cult-Like Buy-In — The agency Kool-Aid, opt in or get out
  • Creativity vs. Capitalism — The soul of advertising
  • Reclamation — Taking back what's been co-opted
  • The Long Con — The slow game being played on multiple levels

Research Needs

  • W+K's internal culture — any public accounts, Glassdoor, industry reporting
  • W+K layoffs during 2023–2024 period
  • Portland cultural dynamics during this era
  • Nike's relationship with W+K — any public tensions or changes
  • W+K's creative output during this period
  • Dan Wieden's legacy and the agency post-founders era

Fictionalization Notes

  • Fictionalized agency name TBD
  • The Nike equivalent in the show — how to handle the biggest brand relationship
  • Portland depiction — real or fictionalized?

TBWA\Chiat\Day

Season 3: The Reckoning

Real Agency Facts

Founded

TBWA in 1970; Chiat\Day in 1968; merged in 1995

Location

Los Angeles, CA (also NYC and global offices)

Size

Large, holding-company-owned (Omnicom)

Reputation

The "Disruption" agency. Known for pushing boundaries and challenging conventions.

Philosophy

"Disruption" — their branded strategic methodology

Notable Campaigns

  • Apple "1984"
  • Gatorade
  • Adidas
  • Nissan
  • PlayStation

Creator's Experience (2024–Present)

Entry Hired as Senior Connection Strategist
Location Los Angeles
Duration April/May 2024 to present
Status Currently employed

Agency Culture

  • Chill nature of corporate America — Less intensity, more ambient work culture
  • "Disruption" as philosophy vs. corporate reality — The irony of a branded disruptive methodology within a holding company machine
  • Part of Omnicom machine — Holding company dynamics shape everything, from strategy to operations
  • LA energy — Less intense office culture, more flexibility, different creative rhythms
  • Post-Covid era — The new normal of hybrid/flexible work
  • Merger looming over everything — Consolidation and uncertainty in the air

Key Stories & Incidents

LA Arrival

The energy shift from Portland. LA "work" culture—different pace, different values, different mythology.

Omnicom Merger Dynamics

What the merger means for the agency, the people, the industry. Corporate consolidation and what gets lost in the process.

Post-Everything Feel

Post-Covid, post-BLM peak, post-cult. A different kind of energy—exhaustion, clarity, pragmatism.

AI Becoming Real

AI tools in meetings, in workflows, in the air. No longer abstract—it's here, it's changing everything, it's the story.

The Strategic Awakening

The protagonist sees the full picture for the first time. The long con becomes visible. The future is now.

Chill Corporate America

The contrast between W+K intensity and TBWA calm. Different kinds of ambition, different ways of winning.

Show Themes Most Present

  • Looming Apocalypse — AI, cultural shifts, industry collapse
  • First-Mover's Advantage — Who sees the future first
  • The Long Con — The slow game being played on multiple levels
  • Reclamation — Taking back what's been co-opted

Research Needs

  • Omnicom-IPG merger details and timeline
  • TBWA\Chiat\Day's current positioning and culture
  • "Disruption" methodology — what it actually means in practice
  • TBWA's client roster and notable recent work
  • LA advertising scene vs. NY/SF/Portland
  • How holding company dynamics affect individual agencies

  • Fictionalized agency name TBD
  • Omnicom equivalent in the show
  • Current employer — extra sensitivity around fictionalization
  • The "Disruption" philosophy — keep, rename, or reimagine?

Thematic Progression

1

GSP — The Reckoning Begins

The protagonist experiences cognitive dissonance as a statement of diversity contradicts internal practice. Performativeness is exposed. The illusion begins to crack.

2

W+K — The Deepening

The protagonist is seduced by myth and intensity, then ejected. The cult logic becomes clear: you're either all in or out. Creativity and capitalism are fundamentally opposed.

3

TBWA — The Awakening Completes

The protagonist sees the full picture: AI, consolidation, the long con, the game being played. They can either participate or disrupt from within.
