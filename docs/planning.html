Open Questions

Questions that need to be resolved to advance the series bible. Prioritized by impact on development.

High Priority (Blocks Series Bible Completion)

Characters

  • Who are the top 5–10 characters and what roles do they play?
  • What is the protagonist's fictionalized name?
  • How closely do characters map to real people? 1:1 or composites?
  • Which characters span multiple agency eras?

Structure

  • How do seasons break down? (Which eras get 1 season vs. 2?)
  • What's the cold open style? (Teaser? Flash-forward? In medias res?)
  • What's the episode structure within the half-hour? (Cold open → Act 1 → Act 2 → Act 3? Or more fluid?)

Names & Legal

  • Fictionalized agency names — what's the approach? (Transparent analogs? Fully fictional?)
  • Legal strategy for depicting real people and places
  • How fictional are the cities? (Real SF/Portland/LA or fictionalized versions?)

Medium Priority (Enriches the Bible)

MAIP

  • Creator wants to explain MAIP's full role beyond "connective tissue" — need that conversation

Story Specifics

  • The "never promoted at GSP" story — what happened?
  • The W+K layoff story — how did it go down?
  • The vaccination incident at GSP — full story
  • What specific moments/memories seed the pilot episode?

Tone & Style

  • Visual style references — what does the show look like? (Cinematography, color palette)
  • Narration? Voice-over? Or purely dramatized?
  • Atlanta-style surreal episodes — are those in play?

Low Priority (Future Development)

World

  • Supporting character arcs — who changes, who stays the same?
  • Recurring motifs or visual symbols
  • How does the show handle social media / digital culture visually?
  • The Go metaphor (Move #37, Move #78) — how does this manifest?

Industry

  • Specific real campaigns or industry moments to reference/fictionalize
  • Client dynamics — do we see the client side?
  • How does the creative process (making ads) get depicted?

Music

  • Beyond the playlist — is music diegetic, scored, or needle-drop heavy?
  • Does the protagonist have a relationship with music?
  • Soundtrack strategy — licensed music budget considerations
