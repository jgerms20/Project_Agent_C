Comp Show Scripts

Purpose: Studying the pilot episodes and key episodes of comparable shows to inform structure, pacing, and tonal approach for The Agency. Each show offers a different lesson in how to open a world, introduce an outsider protagonist, and balance comedy with drama.

Mad Men

AMC · 2007–2015 · Matthew Weiner
Pilot Script

“Smoke Gets in Your Eyes”

Season 1 · Episode 1

The gold standard for advertising-world pilots. How Weiner introduces Don Draper, Sterling Cooper, and the seductive/destructive world of 1960s advertising in a single episode.

Atlanta

FX · 2016–2022 · Donald Glover
Pilot Script

“The Big Bang”

Season 1 · Episode 1

How Glover introduces Earn, Paper Boi, and the surreal-yet-grounded world of Atlanta. The template for our half-hour hybrid format and tonal range.
