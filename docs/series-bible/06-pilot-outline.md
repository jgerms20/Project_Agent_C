## Initial Considerations

### Where Does the Pilot Start?
Options to discuss with the creator:
- [ ] The MAIP notification — the moment the protagonist learns they're entering the industry
- [ ] First day at GSP — the surreal experience of starting a dream job on Zoom during Covid
- [ ] In medias res — start at a later, more dramatic moment and flash back
- [ ] Before advertising — the protagonist's life in the South, the decision to pursue this path

### What Does the Pilot Need to Accomplish?
1. Introduce the protagonist and make us root for them
2. Establish the world of advertising (the allure AND the bullshit)
3. Set the tone — funny, sharp, with undercurrents of something darker
4. Plant seeds for the season's themes (cognitive dissonance, performativeness)
5. End with a hook — something that makes you need to watch Episode 2
6. Establish the show's unique voice and visual style

### Pilot Tone
Should feel like: "This is going to be something special." The audience should feel like they're getting insider access to a world they've never seen, told by someone who actually lived it.
