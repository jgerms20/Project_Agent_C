## Protagonist

### [Name TBD] — Fictionalized Creator
**Agency Era(s)**: All three — GSP → W+K → TBWA
**Role in Story**: Lead / POV character
**Archetype**: The outsider who becomes the one who sees everything

**Who They Are**: A young Black strategist from the South who enters the advertising industry in 2020 through MAIP, right as the world breaks open. Smart, observant, ambitious — but fundamentally an outsider in every room they enter. They see things others miss because they were never fully let inside.

**What Drives Them**: Understanding the system. Then mastering it. Then disrupting it.

**Their Contradiction**: They're critical of the industry's bullshit but also drawn to it. They want to succeed in a system they know is broken.

**Arc (Full Series)**:
- **Season 1 (GSP)**: Eyes wide open. Absorbing everything. Learning the language. Seeing the contradictions but not yet knowing what to do with them.
- **Season 2 (W+K)**: Going deeper. Buying in — or trying to. First time truly inside. The high of belonging and the devastation of being discarded.
- **Season 3+ (TBWA/Beyond)**: Clarity. Strategy. Using everything they've learned — including AI — as a catalyst to build something new.

**Inspired By**: Fictionalized version of the creator. Core experiences are autobiographical; character has room to diverge.

## Character Roles Needed

For a strong ensemble, the show will benefit from characters filling these roles across the three eras:

- **The Mentor** — someone who shows the protagonist the ropes (and later reveals their own compromises)
- **The Rival** — someone competing for the same space, possibly from a different background
- **The Mirror** — someone who reflects what the protagonist could become (for better or worse)
- **The Love Interest** — romantic relationship that complicates the work dynamics
- **The Insider** — someone who's been in the industry forever and is either part of the problem or tired of it
- **The Fellow Outsider** — another MAIP connection or POC navigating the same spaces
- **The True Believer** — someone who genuinely drinks the agency Kool-Aid
- **The Casualty** — someone the industry chews up and spits out
