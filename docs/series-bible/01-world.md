# The World of The Agency

## The Advertising Industry, 2020-2026

The show is set during the most turbulent period in advertising since the Mad Men era. The industry is simultaneously:
- **Reckoning with diversity** — post-George Floyd, agencies scramble to prove they're not what they've always been
- **Surviving a pandemic** — remote work reshapes agency culture overnight
- **Facing an AI apocalypse** — the tools that were supposed to help creatives start replacing them
- **Consolidating** — mergers (like Omnicom) swallow agencies whole
- **Losing its soul** — or maybe admitting it never had one

This is not a show about a thriving industry. This is a show about an industry in decline that doesn't know it's declining — told by someone watching it happen from the inside.

## The Three Worlds

### San Francisco / Goodby Silverstein & Partners Era (2020-2023)
*"The Awakening"*

**The Agency**: One of the most respected independent agencies in the country. Known for smart, sophisticated work. "Got Milk?" legacy. Academic energy — these people think they're the smartest in the room, and they might be. But smart doesn't mean good.

**The City**: San Francisco during/post-Covid. A tech capital that's simultaneously the future and a ghost town. The protagonist experiences it entirely remotely — the city exists as an idea, a Zoom background, a mythology they're not part of.

**The Culture**: Peak performativeness. Diversity statements flying while the actual diverse people get sidelined. The agency says all the right things during BLM and does none of them. The protagonist enters eyes wide open and slowly watches the contradictions pile up.

**The Mood**: Isolation (remote work), observation, slow awakening, cognitive dissonance

---

### Portland / Wieden+Kennedy Era (2023-2024)
*"The Cult"*

**The Agency**: Arguably the most famous advertising agency in the world. Nike's agency. The place where "Just Do It" was born. The mythology is intoxicating — and it's designed to be. You're not just working at an agency; you're joining a movement. Buy in or get out.

**The City**: Portland, Oregon. "Keep Portland Weird." A city that built an identity on being alternative and is watching that identity commercialize in real time. The protagonist — a Black Southerner — navigating this aggressively "open-minded" but very white city.

**The Culture**: Cult-like buy-in at its peak. Corporate culture masquerading as creative revolution. The agency's self-mythology is so strong that questioning it feels like heresy. First time in-person — the protagonist finally experiences what agency life actually feels like, smells like, sounds like.

**The Mood**: Intensity, belonging and alienation simultaneously, creative highs, the axe waiting to fall

---

### Los Angeles / TBWA\Chiat\Day Era (2024-present)
*"The Reckoning"*

**The Agency**: Part of the Omnicom machine. Chill energy that masks corporate machinery. "Disruption" is the agency philosophy, which is ironic given how un-disruptive the day-to-day feels. The Omnicom merger looms over everything.

**The City**: Los Angeles. The entertainment capital of the world. Everyone is working on something. "Work" culture in quotes because the hustle is different here — less intense office culture, more ambient ambition.

**The Culture**: Post-everything. Post-Covid, post-BLM peak, post-the-old-way-of-doing-things. AI is no longer a whisper; it's in the room. The protagonist has seen enough now to understand the game — the question is what to do with that knowledge.

**The Mood**: Clarity, strategy, accumulation, the calm before the protagonist makes their move

## Industry Timeline (Show Context)

| Year | Industry Reality | Show Significance |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|

| 2022 | ChatGPT launches (Nov). Economic slowdown. Layoffs begin. | AI Easter eggs planted. The ground shifts beneath everyone |
| 2023 | AI tools proliferate. More layoffs. Agency consolidation accelerates. | Protagonist moves to W+K. The cult. The in-person reality. |
