The World of The Agency

The advertising industry, 2020–2026 — three agencies, three cities, one broken system

Industry Context

The show is set during the most turbulent period in advertising since the Mad Men era. The industry is simultaneously reckoning, surviving, collapsing, and consolidating — often all at once, often inside the same open-plan office.

  • Reckoning with Diversity Post-George Floyd, agencies scramble to prove they are not what they have always been. The statements come easy. The action does not.
  • Surviving a Pandemic Remote work reshapes agency culture overnight. The rituals, the politics, the social dynamics — all of it migrates to Zoom and Slack and never fully comes back.
  • Facing an AI Apocalypse The tools meant to help creatives start replacing them. The industry keeps pretending otherwise until it cannot.
  • Consolidating Mergers like Omnicom swallow agencies whole. Independence becomes a fiction. Corporate machinery hums beneath the creative mythology.
  • Losing Its Soul Or maybe admitting it never had one. The advertising industry has always sold aspiration while operating on cynicism. Post-2020, the gap between the two becomes impossible to ignore.

This is not a show about a thriving industry. This is a show about an industry in decline that doesn't know it's declining — told by someone watching it happen from the inside.

Three Worlds

Era 1  ·  2020–2023

San Francisco / Goodby Silverstein & Partners

The Awakening ◆ San Francisco, CA  ·  Fully Remote
The Agency

One of the most respected independent agencies in the country. Known for smart, sophisticated work. "Got Milk?" is the legacy. Academic energy — these people think they're the smartest in the room, and they might be. But smart doesn't mean good.

The City

San Francisco during and after Covid. A tech capital that is simultaneously the future and a ghost town. The protagonist experiences it entirely remotely — the city exists as an idea, a Zoom background, a mythology they are not part of.

The Culture

Peak performativeness. Diversity statements flying while the actual diverse people get sidelined. The agency says all the right things during BLM and does none of them. The protagonist enters eyes wide open and slowly watches the contradictions pile up.

Key Tension

Protagonist is hired through MAIP — brought in as a symbol of progress, treated as exactly that. Junior Strategist title. Never promoted. The vaccination incident is the clearest expression of what they are and aren't willing to do for the people they claim to value.

Mood
Isolation Observation Slow Awakening Cognitive Dissonance Remote Distance
Era 2  ·  2023–2024

Portland / Wieden+Kennedy

The Cult ◆ Portland, OR  ·  First In-Person Era
The Agency

Arguably the most famous advertising agency in the world. Nike's agency. The place where "Just Do It" was born. The mythology is intoxicating — and it's designed to be. You're not just working at an agency; you're joining a movement. Buy in or get out.

The City

"Keep Portland Weird." A city that built an identity on being alternative and is watching that identity commercialize in real time. The protagonist — a Black Southerner — navigating this aggressively "open-minded" but very white city for the first time.

The Culture

Cult-like buy-in at its peak. Corporate culture masquerading as creative revolution. The agency's self-mythology is so strong that questioning it feels like heresy. First time in-person: the protagonist finally experiences what agency life actually feels like, smells like, sounds like.

Key Tension

Senior / Comms & Media Supervisor — the highest title yet, surrounded by the most mythology. The belonging is real. So is the alienation. The axe is waiting to fall. The layoff does not come from nowhere; the protagonist saw it before it came.

Mood
Intensity Belonging Alienation Creative Highs The Axe Waiting
Era 3  ·  2024–Present

Los Angeles / TBWA\Chiat\Day

The Reckoning ◆ Los Angeles, CA  ·  Post-Everything
The Agency

Part of the Omnicom machine. Chill energy that masks corporate machinery. "Disruption" is the agency philosophy, which is ironic given how un-disruptive the day-to-day feels. The Omnicom merger looms over everything like weather no one wants to name.

The City

Los Angeles. The entertainment capital. Everyone is working on something. "Work" culture here is ambient — less intense office, more low-key constant hum. People are in the industry and adjacent to ten other industries. Nothing is accidental.

The Culture

Post-everything. Post-Covid, post-BLM peak, post-the-old-way-of-doing-things. AI is no longer a whisper — it's in the room. The protagonist has seen enough across two agencies to understand the game. The question now is what to do with that knowledge.

Key Tension

Senior Connection Strategist — a title that sounds like it could mean anything, and might. The protagonist moves with more clarity and strategy than in either previous era. This is the accumulation phase. The disruptor is taking shape.

Mood
Clarity Strategy Accumulation Calm Before the Move AI Crescendo

Industry Timeline

2020
Industry Reality

Covid hits. Remote work overnight. George Floyd. BLM statements everywhere. Agencies rush to prove they are not what they are.

Show Significance

Protagonist enters the industry at the exact worst and best moment. The contradictions are already baked in.

2021
Industry Reality

"Return to office" debates. Vaccine mandates. DEI hiring surges. Everyone is hiring Black and brown talent — and giving none of them power.

Show Significance

Contradictions deepen. Told they matter while kept at arm's length. The gap between the statement and the reality widens.

2022
Industry Reality

ChatGPT launches in November. Economic slowdown. Layoffs begin across tech and adjacent industries. The floor starts moving.

Show Significance

AI Easter eggs planted. The ground shifts beneath everyone and almost nobody notices in time. Protagonist notices.

2023
Industry Reality

AI tools proliferate. More layoffs. Agency consolidation accelerates. The creative industry's anxiety about irrelevance becomes open and audible.

Show Significance

Protagonist moves to W+K. The cult. The in-person reality hits. Everything they knew about agency culture was theoretical until now.

2024
Industry Reality

Omnicom-IPG merger announced. AI becomes unavoidable. Industry identity crisis accelerates. The old guard starts quietly exiting.

Show Significance

W+K layoff. Move to TBWA\Chiat\Day. The reckoning begins. The protagonist arrives in LA carrying three years of receipts.

2025–26
Industry Reality

AI reshapes everything. Old guard exits. New models emerge. The industry that existed in 2020 is unrecognizable. Or maybe, finally, honest.

Show Significance

Protagonist sees the full picture. The disruptor emerges. The slow game comes into focus. The outside-in movement begins.
