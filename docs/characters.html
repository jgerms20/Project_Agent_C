Who They Are

A young Black strategist from the South who enters the advertising industry in 2020 through MAIP, right as the world breaks open. Smart, observant, ambitious — but fundamentally an outsider in every room they enter. They see things others miss because they were never fully let inside.

What Drives Them

Understanding the system. Then mastering it. Then disrupting it.

Their Contradiction

They’re critical of the industry’s bullshit but also drawn to it. They want to succeed in a system they know is broken.

Arc by Season

  • Season 1 — GSP
    Eyes wide open. Absorbing everything. Learning the language. Seeing the contradictions but not yet knowing what to do with them.
  • Season 2 — W+K
    Going deeper. Buying in — or trying to. First time truly inside. The high of belonging and the devastation of being discarded.
  • Season 3+ — TBWA / Beyond
    Clarity. Strategy. Using everything they’ve learned — including AI — as a catalyst to build something new.
Inspired By Fictionalized version of the creator. Core experiences are autobiographical; character has room to diverge as the story demands.

Character Roles Needed

Narrative functions the ensemble must fill. Not yet assigned to named characters.

Role 01
The Mentor

Shows the protagonist the ropes, then later reveals their own compromises. The cost of longevity in a broken system.

Role 02
The Rival

Competing for the same space, possibly from a different background. Pushes the protagonist to define what they actually want.

Role 03
The Mirror

Reflects what the protagonist could become — better or worse. A living answer to the question: what does this place do to you?

Role 04
The Love Interest

A romantic relationship that complicates the work dynamics. Blurs the already-thin line between professional and personal.

Role 05
The Insider

Has been in the industry forever. Either part of the problem, or exhausted by it — maybe both. Carries institutional memory.

Role 06
The Fellow Outsider

Another MAIP connection or POC navigating the same spaces. A collaborator, confidant, or cautionary tale — depending on the season.

Role 07
The True Believer

Genuinely drinks the agency Kool-Aid. No irony, no distance. Makes the protagonist — and the audience — question their own cynicism.

Role 08
The Casualty

Someone the industry chews up and spits out. A reminder of what’s at stake. The cost of the game, made visible.
