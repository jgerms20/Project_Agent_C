Season 1

The Awakening — Goodby Silverstein & Partners

ERA 1 San Francisco Timeline: 2020–2021 Episodes: TBD (8–10 target)

The protagonist enters advertising through MAIP at the worst/best possible moment — right as Covid hits and the world breaks open. Everything is remote. They've "made it" to one of the most prestigious agencies but experience it entirely through a screen. They watch the industry perform progressive values during BLM while doing nothing. They are the junior strategist who sees everything but can say nothing.

Key Beats
  • The MAIP entry / getting the notification
  • First day at GSP (remote — absurdity of starting a dream job on Zoom)
  • George Floyd / BLM — watching the agency's response in real time
  • The vaccination incident (denied office access)
  • Being junior strategist and never getting promoted
  • Relationships formed remotely — the MAIP cohort as lifeline
  • Discovering the gap between agency mythology and reality
  • Season finale — what pushes them toward leaving?
Tone

Observational. Funny in the way absurd corporate behavior is funny. Lonely in the way remote pandemic work is lonely. Watching, learning, slowly realizing.

Dominant Themes
Cognitive Dissonance Performativeness Cultural + Societal Shifts Allyship vs. Alliance
Season 2

The Cult — Wieden+Kennedy

ERA 2 Portland Timeline: 2023–2024 Episodes: TBD

The protagonist moves to Portland and enters the cult. For the first time, physically inside an agency — everything imagined and nothing like expected. W+K's mythology is intoxicating. The work is legendary. Buy-in is total. Gets swept up, achieves, belongs… then gets laid off. The cult doesn't want them anymore.

Key Beats
  • Arriving in Portland — fish out of water (Southern Black person in whitest city)
  • First day in-person — sensory overload after years of Zoom
  • The W+K mythology — the pitch, the history, the Nike of it all
  • Buying in — the high of belonging
  • "Keep Portland Weird" — navigating Portland's progressive weirdness
  • The work — what they created, what mattered, what got killed
  • Cracks in the cult — seeing behind the curtain
  • The layoff — how it happened, what it felt like, the aftermath
  • Season finale — leaving Portland, heading to LA, changed
Tone

More intense. Higher highs, lower lows. Comedy from cult absurdity; drama from genuine investment betrayed. The layoff episode should be devastating.

Dominant Themes
Cult-Like Buy-In Creativity vs. Capitalism Reclamation The Long Con
Season 3

The Reckoning — TBWA\Chiat\Day

ERA 3 Los Angeles Timeline: 2024–2025 Episodes: TBD

Arrives in LA with scars and clarity. TBWA is chill, corporate, Omnicom machine. Cult energy gone — replaced by corporate entropy. But the protagonist has changed. Sees the full picture: industry, AI, consolidation, the long con. Season is about strategic positioning — making moves.

Key Beats
  • Landing in LA — the energy shift from Portland
  • TBWA culture — "Disruption" philosophy meets corporate reality
  • The Omnicom merger — rumblings, implications
  • AI becoming impossible to ignore — tools in meetings, people losing jobs
  • The protagonist's strategic awakening — using AI, not fearing it
  • Building real alliances
  • The catalyst moment — when the protagonist decides to disrupt
Tone

Calmer surface, deeper currents. Drier comedy. More strategic than emotional. No longer reactive — playing the game.

Dominant Themes
A Looming Apocalypse First-Mover's Advantage The Long Con Reclamation

Non-Linear Elements

The show's timeline is not strictly sequential. Non-linear devices serve thematic rather than narrative purposes — used deliberately, not as a default.

Flashbacks

Protagonist's life before advertising — the South, college, the decision to pursue this world.

Flash-Forwards

Brief glimpses of where things head. Creates dramatic irony — the audience knows what the protagonist doesn't yet.

Blended Timelines

Episodes cutting between eras for thematic parallels. A 2020 moment rhymes with a 2024 one.

MAIP Threads

The MAIP cohort provides continuity across all eras — a through-line that stretches from Season 1 to the end.
