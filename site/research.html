Real agency context and creator experiences
Goodby Silverstein & Partners Era 1

Show Era: Season 1 — "The Awakening"

Real Agency Facts

  • Founded: 1983 by Jeff Goodby and Rich Silverstein
  • Location: San Francisco, CA
  • Known for: "Got Milk?", BMW Films, Doritos, Frito-Lay, Pepsi, HP, Adobe, NBA
  • Reputation: One of the most respected independent agencies. Creative excellence. Smart, sophisticated work.
  • Size: Mid-size independent (vs. holding company agencies)

Creator's Experience (2020-2023)

  • Entry: Through MAIP internship program → brought on full-time
  • Role: Junior Strategist (entire tenure — never promoted)
  • Work mode: Entirely remote. Never lived in SF.
  • Duration: ~3 years (2020 to April/May 2023)

Key Stories / Incidents

  • The vaccine incident: Creator was unvaccinated, denied office access. Story of exclusion disguised as policy.
  • BLM response: Agency's public statements vs. internal reality during George Floyd protests
  • Never promoted: Junior strategist the entire time at a prestigious agency — what happened?
  • Remote disconnect: Experiencing a "dream agency" entirely through screens
  • Entry story: Getting the MAIP notification, the feeling of making it

Agency Culture (To Be Detailed)

  • Sophisticated, academic energy
  • "Very, very smart" — intellectual culture
  • Peak performativeness during Covid + BLM
  • Contradictory: says diversity, contradicts in practice
  • Creator describes as "eyes wide open" awakening

Show Themes Most Present

  • Cognitive Dissonance (primary)
  • Performativeness
  • Allyship vs. Alliance
  • Cultural + Societal Shifts

Research Needs

  • GSP's actual public statements during BLM (2020)
  • GSP's diversity/inclusion initiatives and outcomes
  • Agency leadership during this period
  • Notable campaigns/work during 2020-2023
  • Any public layoffs or restructuring
  • Jeff Goodby's public persona and role during this era

  • How closely to mirror the real agency
  • Which details are changed, which are kept
Wieden+Kennedy Era 2

Show Era: Season 2 — "The Cult"

Real Agency Facts

  • Founded: 1982 by Dan Wieden and David Kennedy
  • Location: Portland, OR (HQ), with offices worldwide
  • Known for: Nike ("Just Do It"), Old Spice, Coca-Cola, Ford, McDonald's
  • Reputation: Arguably the most famous advertising agency in the world. Creative legend. Independent.
  • Size: The largest independently owned agency globally
  • Culture: Famously intense. Creative-first. Strong internal mythology.

Creator's Experience (2023-2024)

  • Entry: Hired as Senior / Comms & Media Supervisor
  • Location: Portland — first time being in-person at an agency
  • Duration: ~1 year (2023-2024)
  • Exit: Laid off — major story beat

Key Stories / Incidents

  • First time in-person: After years of remote work at GSP, finally being physically in an agency. The sensory reality of it.
  • The cult buy-in: How W+K's mythology works on you. The history, the Nike of it all.
  • Portland culture shock: A Black Southerner navigating "Keep Portland Weird"
  • The layoff: How it happened, when, who else, the feeling, the aftermath
  • The work: What was created, what mattered, what got killed by clients/politics
  • Belonging and alienation: The high of being inside the most famous agency, the simultaneous outsider feeling

Agency Culture (To Be Detailed)

  • Height of cult-like vibes
  • "Buy in or opt out" mentality
  • Corporate culture masquerading as creative revolution
  • The agency's self-mythology is powerful and deliberate
  • Nike relationship as the agency's crown jewel and identity
  • "Keep Portland Weird" extends into the agency itself

Show Themes Most Present

  • Cult-Like Buy-In (primary)
  • Creativity vs. Capitalism
  • Reclamation
  • The Long Con

Research Needs

  • W+K's internal culture — any public accounts, Glassdoor, industry reporting
  • W+K layoffs during 2023-2024 period
  • Portland cultural dynamics during this era
  • Nike's relationship with W+K — any public tensions or changes
  • W+K's creative output during this period
  • Dan Wieden's legacy and the agency post-founders era

  • Fictionalized agency name TBD
  • The Nike equivalent in the show — how to handle the biggest brand relationship
  • Portland depiction — real or fictionalized?
TBWA\Chiat\Day Era 3

Show Era: Season 3 — "The Reckoning"

Real Agency Facts

  • Founded: TBWA in 1970; Chiat\Day in 1968; merged in 1995
  • Location: Los Angeles, CA (also NYC and global offices)
  • Known for: Apple "1984" ad, Gatorade, Adidas, Nissan, PlayStation, Disruption methodology
  • Reputation: The "Disruption" agency. Part of the Omnicom Group.
  • Size: Large, holding-company-owned (Omnicom)
  • Philosophy: "Disruption" — their branded strategic methodology

Creator's Experience (2024-present)

  • Entry: Hired as Senior Connection Strategist
  • Location: Los Angeles
  • Duration: April/May 2024 to present
  • Status: Currently employed

Key Stories / Incidents

  • LA arrival: The energy shift from Portland. LA "work" culture.
  • Omnicom merger dynamics: What the merger means for the agency, the people, the industry
  • Post-everything feel: Post-Covid, post-BLM peak, post-cult. A different kind of energy.
  • AI becoming real: AI tools in meetings, in workflows, in the air. No longer abstract.
  • The strategic awakening: The protagonist sees the full picture for the first time
  • Chill corporate America: The contrast between W+K intensity and TBWA calm

Agency Culture (To Be Detailed)

  • Chill nature of corporate America
  • "Disruption" as philosophy vs. corporate reality (irony)
  • Part of Omnicom machine — the holding company dynamics
  • LA energy — less intense office culture, more ambient
  • Post-Covid era — the new normal of hybrid/flexible work
  • Merger looming over everything

Show Themes Most Present

  • Looming Apocalypse (primary)
  • First-Mover's Advantage
  • The Long Con
  • Reclamation

Research Needs

  • Omnicom-IPG merger details and timeline
  • TBWA\Chiat\Day's current positioning and culture
  • "Disruption" methodology — what it actually means in practice
  • TBWA's client roster and notable recent work
  • LA advertising scene vs. NY/SF/Portland
  • How holding company dynamics affect individual agencies
